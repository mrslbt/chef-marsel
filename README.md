# Chef Marsel

A precision cooking guide that scales Japanese recipes by ingredient weight.

**[Open the app](https://mrslbt.github.io/chef-marsel/)**

---

## What it does

You pick a dish, enter how many grams of the main ingredient you have, and the app recalculates every single ingredient proportionally — down to the gram, down to the spoon.

Built for cooking in Itabashi, Tokyo, using Japanese measuring spoons (大さじ / 小さじ / 2.5 SPOON).

## How to use

1. Open the app on your phone
2. Select a dish
3. Enter the weight of your main ingredient (e.g. 170g instead of 200g)
4. Everything scales — inventory, sauce, spoon measurements

### Add to home screen (iOS)

Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. It works as a standalone app with a custom icon.

## Stack
